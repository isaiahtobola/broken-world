Kintsugi — mend the broken world

The Japanese art of repairing pottery with gold. Broken is not the end of the story — the seams are where the light gets in, and where the gold goes.

🖱️ ON PCClick a shard and drag it toward the glowing outline. When it settles into place, keep holding the click — the seam heats up, and when the ring fills, the gold sets. Let go too early and it stays loose.
📱 ON PHONETouch a shard and drag it home with your finger. Keep your finger pressed down while the seam heats — hold until the ring fills and the gold sets. Mend every shard and the pot stands again.
The pot lies broken on the floor. Mend it.
